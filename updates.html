Kicker League: What's New

Open Kicker League

October 22, 2026: First version

  • Fantasy football with kickers only. Leagues of 6, 8, or 10 teams, each with 2 starters and 1 bench kicker.
  • Roto scoring over the season: field goals made, 50+ yard field goals, extra points, FG%, XP%, and average made-FG distance. An optional Clutch FGs category counts go-ahead or tying kicks in the last two minutes or overtime.
  • Anyone can create a league and invite friends with a code. The commissioner can pick a custom code like KICKERS26.
  • Lineups lock one kicker at a time, when that kicker's game kicks off.
  • Free agent pickups, and waivers each Wednesday morning, where the team lowest in the standings picks first.
  • Leg Index ranks kickers by how far above or below average they are in every category. You can view stats from the last three seasons or a 3-year blend.
  • Snake draft with a pick clock from 30 seconds up to 24 hours, or no time limit for drafting over several days. Includes Autodraft and a private draft queue.
  • Badges for rare kicker moments: 🎯 an onside kick his team recovers, or 🎩 a fake field goal where the kicker runs or throws for a first down or touchdown. The fantasy team with that kicker gets the badge on the standings and in Activity. Badges are bragging rights only.
  • Live stats during games. While games are on, stats update about every minute, with a Live badge, each kicker's score and game clock, and his kicks so far that week. Official stats replace the live numbers overnight.
